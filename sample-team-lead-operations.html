WORK SAMPLE 04

Team Lead & Operations Support

A fictionalized example based on the type of team leadership, performance monitoring, workflow coordination, and operational support involved in a customer service environment.

01

Team Scenario

A customer support team is experiencing increased ticket volume, longer response times, and a growing backlog. The goal is to maintain service quality while helping the team stay organized, meet performance expectations, and resolve customer issues efficiently.

02

How I Would Approach It

Monitor Team Performance

Review response times, ticket volume, backlog, and overall service quality to identify areas that need attention.

Prioritize Work

Help organize incoming requests based on urgency, complexity, customer impact, and available team capacity.

Support Team Members

Provide guidance on difficult cases, answer process-related questions, and help team members handle escalations properly.

Identify Workflow Gaps

Look for recurring issues, unnecessary steps, and common customer problems that may be contributing to delays or repeated contacts.

Escalate & Follow Through

Make sure complex issues are properly documented, escalated to the appropriate team, and followed through until resolved.

03

Sample Performance Tracking

Area Observation Action
Response Time Increasing during peak volume Review workload and prioritization
Backlog Older cases remaining open Prioritize aging tickets
Escalations Repeated complex issues Identify recurring issue patterns
Service Quality Inconsistent handling Provide coaching and guidance
04

Example Team Guidance

When ticket volume increases, prioritize urgent and customer-impacting cases first while keeping older unresolved tickets visible.

For complex cases, document the issue clearly before escalating so the next team has the information needed to investigate without unnecessary back-and-forth.

If the same issue appears repeatedly, flag the pattern so the team can determine whether a process update, documentation change, or additional guidance may help.

05

What This Demonstrates

Team Leadership

Supporting team members, providing guidance, and helping maintain consistent service standards.

Performance Monitoring

Reviewing workload, response times, backlog, and service quality to identify areas for improvement.

Workflow Improvement

Identifying recurring issues and workflow gaps that can affect efficiency and customer experience.

Escalation Management

Making sure complex cases are properly documented, escalated, and followed through to resolution.

06

Skills Demonstrated

Team Leadership Performance Monitoring Workflow Management Escalation Handling Process Improvement Team Support Documentation Operations Support
