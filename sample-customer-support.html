WORK SAMPLE 01

Customer Support Case

A representative example of how I approach customer inquiries, investigate issues, communicate solutions, and follow through.

Customer Issue

“I received my order, but the item I received is not the one I ordered. Can you help me get this corrected?”

How I Would Handle It

1. Review the order
Verify the order details, product information, and available customer records.

2. Confirm the issue
Identify what was ordered versus what was received and determine the appropriate resolution.

3. Communicate clearly
Explain the next steps to the customer in a clear, professional, and reassuring manner.

4. Process the resolution
Coordinate the appropriate return, replacement, or other approved solution.

5. Document and follow up
Update the case information and follow up when necessary to ensure the issue is fully resolved.

Sample Customer Response

Hi [Customer Name],

I'm sorry about the mix-up with your order. I'd be happy to help get this resolved.

I've reviewed the issue and can assist with the appropriate replacement or return process. Please confirm the item you received so I can make sure we provide the correct next step.

Thank you for your patience, and I'll make sure we get this taken care of.
