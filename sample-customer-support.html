WORK SAMPLE 01

E-commerce Customer Support

A fictionalized example based on the type of customer support cases I have handled in e-commerce environments, including order issues, returns, replacements, and customer follow-ups.

01

Customer Issue

“I received my order, but the item I received is not the one I ordered. Can you help me get this corrected?”

02

How I Handle It

Review the Order

Verify the order details, product information, shipping information, and available customer records.

Confirm the Issue

Compare what the customer ordered with what was received and determine the appropriate resolution.

Communicate Clearly

Explain the situation and next steps clearly while keeping the communication professional and reassuring.

Resolve the Issue

Coordinate the appropriate return, replacement, refund, or other approved solution based on the situation.

Document & Follow Up

Update the case information, document the resolution, and follow up when necessary to ensure the issue is fully resolved.

03

Sample Customer Response

Hi [Customer Name],

I'm sorry about the mix-up with your order. I'd be happy to help get this resolved.

I've reviewed the issue and can assist with the appropriate replacement or return process. Please confirm the item you received so I can make sure we provide the correct next step.

Thank you for your patience, and I'll make sure we get this taken care of.

04

What This Demonstrates

Customer Communication

Clear, professional, and empathetic communication throughout the support process.

Issue Resolution

Identifying the problem and determining the appropriate resolution efficiently.

Order Support

Handling order, shipping, return, replacement, and product-related inquiries.

Documentation & Follow-up

Keeping case information organized and following through until the customer's issue is resolved.

Skills Demonstrated
