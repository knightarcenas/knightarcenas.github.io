WORK SAMPLE 02

Technical Support

A fictionalized example based on the type of technical support situations I have handled, including troubleshooting, customer guidance, issue investigation, and escalation.

01

Reported Issue

“I'm unable to access my account even after resetting my password. I've tried several times, but I still can't log in.”

02

Troubleshooting Process

Verify the Issue

Review the customer's account information and gather details about the login problem, including any error messages or recent changes.

Identify Possible Causes

Determine whether the issue may be related to the password reset, account status, browser, device, or another known access issue.

Guide the Customer

Provide clear, step-by-step troubleshooting instructions appropriate to the customer's situation.

Test the Resolution

Confirm whether the recommended steps resolve the issue before moving to additional troubleshooting or escalation.

Escalate When Necessary

If the issue requires access or action outside the support role, escalate the case with complete notes and relevant troubleshooting details.

03

Sample Customer Response

Hi [Customer Name],

I'm sorry you're having trouble accessing your account. I'd be happy to help you troubleshoot this.

First, please confirm whether you're seeing a specific error message when attempting to sign in. If possible, please also try accessing your account using a different browser or device to help determine whether the issue is device-specific.

If you're still unable to access the account after trying these steps, I'll gather the relevant details and make sure the issue is properly escalated for further assistance.

Thank you for your patience.

04

Escalation Notes

Issue: Customer unable to access account after password reset.

Troubleshooting: Password reset attempted. Customer advised to verify error message and test access through another browser or device.

Status: Pending further investigation.

Next Action: Escalate with relevant account and troubleshooting details if the issue persists.

05

What This Demonstrates

Troubleshooting

Breaking down a technical issue and working through logical troubleshooting steps.

Problem Solving

Identifying possible causes and determining the appropriate next action.

Customer Guidance

Explaining technical steps in a simple and professional way that customers can understand.

Escalation Handling

Providing complete information when an issue needs to be escalated to another team or support level.

06

Skills Demonstrated

Technical Support Troubleshooting Problem Solving Customer Communication Issue Investigation Escalation Handling Documentation Follow-up
