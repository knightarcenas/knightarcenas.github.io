WORK SAMPLE 03

Administrative Support

A fictionalized example based on the administrative tasks commonly involved in customer support and remote operations, including documentation, organization, data handling, and workflow coordination.

01

Task Scenario

A support team is handling a high volume of customer requests. Several cases require updates, follow-ups, accurate documentation, and coordination so that unresolved issues do not get overlooked.

02

How I Would Handle It

Organize the Work

Review open requests, identify pending actions, and organize tasks according to priority, urgency, and required follow-up.

Update Records

Maintain accurate customer or task records, including status, notes, actions taken, and important follow-up information.

Manage Follow-ups

Track pending responses and follow-up dates to help ensure outstanding requests continue moving toward resolution.

Coordinate Information

Gather relevant information and communicate updates between customers, support staff, and other teams when necessary.

Maintain Accuracy

Double-check entries, notes, and task status to reduce errors and keep information reliable and easy to reference.

03

Sample Task Tracking

Task Priority Status Next Action
Customer follow-up High Pending Follow up with customer
Order issue High In Progress Verify resolution
Documentation update Normal Pending Update records
Internal request Normal Completed No further action
04

Sample Internal Note

Status: Follow-up required.

Action Taken: Reviewed the request, confirmed the available information, and updated the task record.

Next Step: Follow up with the appropriate contact and update the record once a response is received.

Priority: Normal

05

What This Demonstrates

Organization

Keeping multiple tasks, requests, and follow-ups organized and prioritized.

Data & Record Management

Maintaining accurate records and keeping information current and easy to reference.

Administrative Coordination

Managing information and coordinating tasks between customers, team members, and other departments.

Attention to Detail

Reviewing information carefully and maintaining accurate documentation to reduce errors.

06

Skills Demonstrated

Administrative Support Data Entry Documentation Task Management Follow-up Record Keeping Organization Attention to Detail
